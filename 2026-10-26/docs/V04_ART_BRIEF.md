# v0.4 visual target: a readable coastal diorama

This brief precedes new asset production. Preserve the bright coastal setting and the four adult workers' established identities. The target is a cohesive painted adventure scene, with characters and tactical information carrying the strongest silhouettes.

## Composition and hierarchy

- Field mode fills the actual play area with the world. The harbor background painting belongs to harbor/title/result scenes; it must not compete around the field margins.
- Warm pale limestone and muted timber form broad quiet floor shapes. Water is turquoise with broad moving bands. Use dark teal occluding wall faces and continuous raised-edge shadows to distinguish low ground, dry high routes and blocked cells.
- Place architectural masses and landmarks around real encounter routes. The landing, central delivery area and optional northern salvage area need distinct silhouettes and openings. Avoid uniform plazas and a single thin straight pier for the entire district.
- Danger uses a solid edge, directional shapes and a readable symbol, with warm coral as a secondary signal. Cargo uses warm gold. Current action/selection uses cool white-teal. Decorative flowers, ropes and glints remain quieter than enemies, chests and exits.
- Screen information uses a shallow dark teal instrument strip, warm ivory text, compact numerals and a single gold contextual action. Paper cards are reserved for reading and planning, not every HUD fragment.

## Scale and production

At 390x844, the board should show about 7-9 columns and a materially larger vertical view than v0.3, with a recognizable player silhouette and visible approach lanes. Choose final zoom from an operated scene, not a screenshot enlarged to look impressive. The primary field target is at least 500px high; all primary touch targets remain at least 44 CSS pixels.

Retain Noto Sans CJK JP and its license. HUD body/primary values target 14-18px with opaque backing; small captions must not carry unique safety information. Reduce repeated framing, drop shadows and ornamental separators in field mode.

Do not mass-generate tiles or character families before validating the compact quay encounter and new HUD together. Existing normalized assets may be reused during layout/play redesign. For any generated replacement, record its prompt, tool, source, alpha/pivot/crop checks and actual imported use. Inspect game-scale visibility and geometry agreement.
