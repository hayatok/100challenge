A single wide horizontal photographic production design concept for an original Japanese real-time 3D game. Osaka, an ordinary working night in 2026 in a divergent society that literally built the future imagined at Expo '70. The subject is the entrance of a neighbourhood public bath standing immediately beyond a short postwar urban canal bridge. Moderate 32 mm perspective at an adult pedestrian's eye level from the bridge. Foreground waist-high terrazzo bridge pillars with three steel horizontal rails catch coloured light, a narrow dark working canal with wet masonry below. Across the bridge, a real small ground-floor shopfront in a three-storey row house, about 5 metres wide, battered sea-green small square tile, a wine-violet split noren with a single large cream hiragana ゆ, rippled wire-glass sliding panels and fluted milk-glass canopy lamp. Integrated into the glazed vestibule at eye level is a small deep-faced curved CRT behind convex smoked glass, its amber/violet halo shows a live Japanese singer in a cabaret. Two residents in everyday work clothes with bath baskets glance at the SAME broadcast while removing their shoes. A separate small square of backlit milk glass at the door, round speaker grille below it, fat cloth wire runs into conduit. A thin film of early autumn rain marks stone steps, dry dusty spots in protected corners, rubber mat darkened where sandals turn. A wooden crate holding empty returnable milk bottles beside the curtain. The upstairs windows have laundry and one warm kitchen light. The technology is materially rooted in mass-produced 1968-1974 Japanese municipal equipment lovingly repaired over five decades, no modern smart screens. Night with realistic exposure, warm entrance pools, a little cobalt and dull magenta from an out-of-frame theatre sign across the water, believable fine ceramic glazes and wool, darks retain detail. Human-scale lived-in Osaka, not a science-fiction megacity, no flying cars, no cyberpunk kanji collage, no modern brand, no graphic overlay, no caption, no border. Balanced legible image for environment artists to build the threshold and bridge with convincing scale and a path a player can actually walk.
